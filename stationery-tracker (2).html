How to Use StatioTrack Pro
STEP 01
✏️
Enter Product Name
Type any stationery item name. The app auto-detects the product type and shows a matching icon instantly.
STEP 02
🔢
Set Daily Quantity
Enter how many units you typically sell in one day. A live profit preview shows before you submit.
STEP 03
🏷️
Enter Both Prices
Set your buying (original) cost and your selling price. Daily profit is calculated automatically for you.
STEP 04
📊
Read Your Dashboard
The 5 cards at the top update instantly with daily totals, your best seller, and profit margin.
STEP 05
📅
Check 7-Day History
Switch to the "7-Day History" tab to see all past entries grouped by day, with daily summaries.
STEP 06
🕐
Submission Timestamps
Every product is stamped with the exact time it was added, visible in the table and the history view.
STEP 07
🔍
Search & Sort
Use the search box to filter products. Click any column header to sort ascending or descending.
STEP 08
💾
Auto-Saved Data
All data is saved to your browser automatically. Close and reopen — your records are still there.
